# NPA-ECM — CFO Executive Summary (One Page)

**Date:** June 2026 · **Classification:** Confidential · **FX:** ₦1,388 = $1 USD

---

## Decision

Approve **on-premise NPA Enterprise Content Management (ECM)** — **fixed ₦350M** covers **all ~3,000 NPA employees** (no per-seat license). Compare against commercial ECM where cost **multiplies with every user added**.

---

## Investment requested — **same price at any scale**

| | **Users onboarded** | **Project** (18 mo + 12 mo support) | **5-Year TCO** | **Cost per user / 5 yr** |
|--|--------------------:|--------------------------------------:|---------------:|-------------------------:|
| Phased rollout | 840 | **₦350M** | **₦470M – ₦550M** | ₦560K – ₦655K |
| **Full NPA** | **3,000** | **₦350M** | **₦470M – ₦550M** | **₦157K – ₦183K** |

**Key point:** NPA-ECM total **does not change** from 840 → 3,000 users. Commercial vendors charge **per seat** — their totals **triple or more**.

---

## Annual subscription run-rate (why per-seat hurts at scale)

| | **NPA-ECM** (amortized) | **Laserfiche @ $93** | **M365 E3 @ $39** |
|--|------------------------:|---------------------:|------------------:|
| 840 users | ~₦70M/yr | ~₦1.30B/yr | ~₦546M/yr |
| 3,000 users | **~₦70M/yr** (same) | **~₦4.65B/yr** | **~₦1.95B/yr** |

One year of Laserfiche for 3,000 staff (**~₦4.65B**) ≈ **10×** NPA-ECM’s entire **5-year** budget.

---

## What ₦350M buys (all staff, not 840 only)

- Unlimited internal NPA users (role-based access, not seat licenses)
- Office routing, executive seals, correspondence, DMS, FOIA — **built for NPA**
- On-premise sovereign hosting · HQ + **all ports**
- Training, helpdesk, security audit, **12-month support** — in fixed cap

---

## Recommendation

**Approve ₦350,000,000 fixed** — accommodates **all 3,000 NPA employees** without license uplift. Reject per-seat commercial ECM: at national headcount, **₦10B–₦35B+** over 5 years vs **₦470M–₦550M**.
