# Training Curriculum — NPA-ECM

**Duration:** 12-month helpdesk period with refresher modules (BOQ §4.01–4.02)

---

## Module 1 — Foundation (all users, ½ day)

- Login, MFA, role switcher, My Workspace navigation  
- My Inbox / My Sent, document links, verify seal  
- Help & helpdesk ticket submission (`/helpdesk`)

## Module 2 — Registry officers (1 day)

- Register correspondence (internal/external)  
- Office inbox routing, minute/approve basics  
- Content capture hub, physical records check-in/out  

## Module 3 — Management & approvals (½ day)

- Executive approvals, parallel routing, delegation  
- Analytics dashboards, division/port performance  
- Records governance: retention, legal hold, eDiscovery export  

## Module 4 — ICT & administration (1 day)

- Users & roles, permission matrix, environment parity  
- Integrations hub (email, ERP, webhooks)  
- Legacy import (`import_legacy_records`), DRM policies, audit compliance bundles  

## Module 5 — Train-the-trainer (port leads, 1 day)

- Deliver Modules 1–2 at port; local SOP customization  
- Escalation paths, helpdesk SLAs, hypercare first 30 days  

---

## Assessment

- Practical: register 3 items, minute 1 item, export audit sample  
- Pass threshold: 80% checklist completion observed by trainer
