## 📋 Difference Between "My Documents" and "Document Management"

### My Documents (`/documents`)
**Purpose:** Personal document workspace

**Description:** "Manage documents you own or have access to within your division and organisation"

**Features:**
- ✅ Personal view of your documents
- ✅ Documents you own
- ✅ Documents shared with you
- ✅ Documents in your division/department
- ✅ Simple filtering (status, type, workspace)
- ✅ Quick access to your work
- ✅ Focused on individual productivity

**Use Case:**
- "What documents do I need to work on?"
- "What documents have been shared with me?"
- "What documents are in my division?"

**Target User:** Individual users managing their personal document workspace

---

### Document Management (`/dms`)
**Purpose:** Central ECM workspace

**Description:** "Central workspace for all ECM documents, templates, and collaboration"

**Features:**
- ✅ Organization-wide document repository
- ✅ All documents in the system (with permissions)
- ✅ Workspaces for collaboration
- ✅ Advanced filtering and search
- ✅ Bulk operations
- ✅ Document templates
- ✅ Version management
- ✅ Sharing and permissions
- ✅ Quick stats and analytics
- ✅ Full ECM capabilities

**Use Case:**
- "What documents exist in the organization?"
- "I need to find documents across all divisions"
- "I want to create a workspace for a project"
- "I need to manage document templates"
- "I want to see organization-wide document statistics"

**Target User:** Document managers, administrators, users who need organization-wide access

---

## 🔑 Key Differences

| Feature | My Documents | Document Management |
|---------|-------------|---------------------|
| **Scope** | Personal/Division | Organization-wide |
| **Focus** | Your documents | All documents |
| **Complexity** | Simple, focused | Advanced, comprehensive |
| **Use Case** | Personal productivity | Organization management |
| **Filtering** | Basic | Advanced with workspaces |
| **Bulk Operations** | Limited | Full support |
| **Templates** | View only | Create and manage |
| **Analytics** | Personal stats | Organization stats |
| **Collaboration** | View shared docs | Full workspace collaboration |

## 📝 Notes

### Permissions
- **My Documents** - Available to all users
- **Document Management** - Requires `canAccessDocumentManagement` permission
- **Content Capture** - Requires `canAccessDocumentManagement` permission
- **Records Management** - Requires `canAccessDocumentManagement` permission
- **Advanced Search** - Available to all users
- **Integration Hub** - Requires `canAccessAdministration` permission
